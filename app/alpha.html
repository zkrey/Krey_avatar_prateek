Step 1 · Add your photos

Up to 5 — Krey picks you

The capture set is up to 5 photos. For a real test, follow the set below — Krey keeps only your best frames and ignores anyone else in shot.

Your 5 — the set that tests well
  1. Face selfie — clear, front-on, even light, no sunglasses or hat
  2. Second face — a different day or angle (helps recognition)
  3. Full body — front — head to toe, straight-on, arms slightly out
  4. Full body — side — 90° profile, same distance
  5. Full body — ¾ turn — between front and side
Every shot: plain background, even light, whole subject in frame, fitted clothing. Full-body shots should be tall/portrait. Random screenshots, memes or group photos won't test well — they'll be flagged as you add them.
＋ Add height for measurements in cmoptional

Shape & proportions need no input. Height is the one number that unlocks absolute measurements — and lets Krey cross-check the read against you.
