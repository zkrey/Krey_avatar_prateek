Krey Tester

Team QA tool. Twin uploads a photo to the real backend and shows the extracted pointers (Monk skin tone, hair, eye, body measurements). Fit runs the rule engine offline. Photos are processed then discarded (derive-and-discard) — nothing is stored.

API base:

Face & skin

A clear front-face selfie → skin tone (Monk), hair colour + texture, eye colour, recognition score.

Body measurements

A full-body front photo + your declared vitals → per-part measurements, body shape, accuracy ledger. (Needs the pose model on the server.)
